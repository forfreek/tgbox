**TGsearch rejects illegal content, pornography, gambling, scams and illicit services. These requirements apply to channels, groups and bots.**

Before submitting, check the published content, the functions a bot provides and the websites promoted in descriptions or messages. Names, descriptions and actual use must meet the requirements.

## Content we reject

- **Pornography and sexual services.** This includes pornographic images or videos, promotion of sexual services, and sexual content involving minors.
- **Gambling and betting.** This includes casinos, betting services, gambling affiliates, promotion of gambling platforms and bots providing these services.
- **Scams and phishing.** This includes fake support accounts, fraudulent investments, deceptive payment requests, account or login-code theft, and tools or websites used for fraud.
- **Personal-data and account abuse.** This includes selling leaked personal information, account theft and providing accounts or payment accounts for illegal activities.
- **Malicious tools and illegal trade.** This includes malware distribution, account-theft or network-attack services, and trade in prohibited goods or services such as drugs, illegal firearms and forged documents.
- **Infringement and other prohibited content.** This includes unauthorized distribution of paid content, disclosure of private information, violent threats and other content that violates applicable law or Telegram rules.

Directories, channels, groups and bots that direct users to these activities are also rejected. Changing a name or description leaves the actual content subject to the same review. Paid promotions must meet these requirements too.

## Basic submission requirements

Entries that meet the content rules must also meet these conditions.

- **A public link.** Submit a channel, group or bot through `t.me/username` or `@username`. Private invite links and personal accounts are excluded.
- **Working access.** The public profile must open, the bot must function, and the entry must be available.
- **A clear name and description.** Explain the content or function. Categories and tags should match what the entry provides.
- **Actual content or functionality.** Empty channels, long-abandoned channels, ad-only entries and entries based on fake followers or engagement are rejected.
- **A unique submission.** Search the directory first. Entries already listed or awaiting review do not need another submission.

## How to submit

Open the [submission guide](/en/enroll/) and use this site's bot to send the public link. Check the fetched name, description and type, choose a category and optional tags, then confirm your submission.

Regular listing is free. The bot sends the review result by private message. Approved entries appear with the next website update. Check the bot's messages to see whether submissions are available and whether any limits apply.

If a submission is rejected, read the reason and address the problem before following the next instructions. Repeated submissions remain subject to the same content review.

## Reporting problems after listing

We review existing entries when prohibited content, broken links or changes of use are found. Depending on the issue, we may correct the information or remove the entry. The same requirement applies to regular and promoted listings.

Send `/support` to this site's bot and provide the following information.

- The username and directory entry URL.
- The problem and relevant public message or webpage links.
- The reason for the correction, removal or report.

Submissions must also comply with [Telegram's Terms of Service](https://telegram.org/tos).
